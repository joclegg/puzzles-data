# Word list

The answers come from 12dicts 6.0.2 by Alan Beale (http://wordlist.aspell.net/12dicts/), released to the public domain: the words of 3 to 15 letters in both `3esl.txt` and `3of6game.txt`. `sources/crossword/words.txt` holds them in uppercase.

The clues in `clues.txt` were written by Claude.
